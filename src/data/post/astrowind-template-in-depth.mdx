Current medical guidelines for cardiovascular risk are based on research from mostly European people and are not always suitable for South Asians and can lead to differences in the level of healthcare received.
The South Asian Genes and Health in Australia (SAGHA) Study aims to address the lack of representation of South Asians in cardiovascular genetics research,
with the long-term goal of improving medical guidelines for preventing and treating cardiovascular disease for this population.

More information can be found at https://saghaus.org/
