HSU achieved international biobanking certification from the Canadian Tissue Repository Network (CTRNet). CTRNet certification is an internationally recognised certification available to biobanks who successfully demonstrate operations that align with biobanking governance, best practices and policies.

Congratulations to Leanne Wallace, our Laboratory Manager who led this initiative and the whole HSU team who have contributed to this achievement.
